# Hi, I'm Susmit 👋

I build things, break things, and occasionally turn the resulting questions into papers.

These days I work on language models, privacy-focused AI systems, reasoning, evaluation, and the reliability of the artifacts we build research on. Outside work, I spend an unreasonable amount of time running large open-weight models locally, patching inference stacks, messing with quantization, and generally finding out what consumer hardware will tolerate.

Before all of that, I built combat robots.

## What I do

I am a Privacy Research Scientist (AI) at PrivaSapien. My work has included multi-agent systems, document-grounded assistants, privacy-preserving inference, privacy threat modelling, and applied research around trustworthy AI systems.

Separately, I do independent research when something bothers me enough that I want to test it properly.

## The home lab rabbit hole

I run a mixed four-GPU workstation for local models, coding agents, experiments, and the occasional bad idea that becomes a weekend project.

That has led me into custom `llama.cpp` work, low-bit quantization, model compatibility fixes, mixed-precision experiments, and a constrained MoE expert-mix optimizer.

Mostly, I just enjoy this stuff.
